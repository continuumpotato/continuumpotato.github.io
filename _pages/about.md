I am a senior research engineer in structural mechanics and composites at Saint-Gobain Research North America, where I work in Aerospace R&D. I build numerical and analytical models, calibrate them against mechanical test data, and use them to drive design decisions on production aerospace parts.

My current work includes:

- **Load-bearing aircraft windshields.** Parametrized 3D finite-element models (ABAQUS, Python) to define load cases, identify the critical design parameters, and evaluate how manufacturing defects affect performance, validated against mechanical tests on windshield sections.
- **Knitted composites.** Analytical and numerical material models for the anisotropic behavior of thermoplastic composites reinforced with knitted fibers, calibrated with a dedicated test program and used to redesign a manufactured part at 50% lower weight.
- **Material screening.** A test pipeline linking three global manufacturing plants with the R&D center to speed up the evaluation of new materials.

I received my PhD in Mechanical Engineering & Applied Mechanics from the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.me.upenn.edu/) in 2023. My dissertation, _Mechanics-Informed Optimization for Enhanced Adhesion and Toughness_, used gradient-based shape optimization, neural-network surrogates and simulated annealing to design adhesive pillars, lap joints and disordered attachment structures with better adhesion. I also hold an MSE in Materials Science & Engineering from Penn and a B.Tech in Metallurgical & Materials Engineering from [NIT Tiruchirappalli](https://www.nitt.edu/).

**Interests:** finite element analysis, constitutive and material modeling, solid and fracture mechanics, adhesion mechanics, design optimization, and mechanical testing.
